The goal was to make it easier for prospective photography clients to understand Lindsay's services and move from browsing to booking. The site brings portfolio examples, service information, pricing and scheduling into a connected experience.

The website design emphasizes the photography itself while keeping the service details easy to find on a phone. The booking flow gives visitors a direct next step after reviewing the photographer's work. This is a live project; no unverified conversion or booking-growth claims are included.
