Rare Treats 518 needed a small-business website that could present a changing range of treats without treating every offering as permanent inventory. The design focuses on the brand's personality and gives visitors ways to learn about custom orders and local markets.

The website uses a mobile-first layout, with clear paths to custom-order information and market details around Troy, New York. This project illustrates how a local business website can support an evolving product offering without relying on a fixed online catalog.
